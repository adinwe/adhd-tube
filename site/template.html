About ADHD Tube

What this is

A shortlist of YouTube videos about ADHD that people in ADHD communities kept sharing with each other. It works like a filter that counts thousands of people's "watch this one" comments.

Where it comes from

How videos are ranked

By the number of different people who recommended a video, and across how many separate threads. Each person counts once per video, so one keen fan can't push a video up. Views, likes and YouTube's algorithm play no part.

What the labels mean

Expert: from a clinician or researcher, or based on mainstream research. Lived experience: people sharing their own ADHD life. Check the claims: the video makes contested claims or sells something. An AI model wrote these labels and the one-line summaries from each video's title, description and comments, so they can be wrong.

What this isn't

It isn't medical advice, and a popular video isn't automatically a correct one. For diagnosis or treatment, talk to your own clinician. Videos belong to their creators and play on YouTube. This site isn't connected to YouTube, Reddit or the creators.

Feedback

Know a video that should be here, or one that shouldn't? Send a suggestion or feedback. It takes a minute and you don't need to give your name.

Privacy and open data

Your saved and watched lists stay in this browser. We count visits anonymously with Vercel Web Analytics: no cookies, no personal data. Download the full list as a spreadsheet (counts and labels only, no usernames or comments).
